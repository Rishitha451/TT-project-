*Action:* Start on the main dashboard (`/app/dashboard`).
*Narration:* "This is the AI Finance Advisor, a financial management platform extended with investment intelligence capabilities."

### 0:20–0:45 | Investment Snapshot
*Action:* Scroll down to the Investment Snapshot section on the dashboard.
*Narration:* "Investment intelligence is integrated into the main dashboard through a lightweight investment snapshot."

### 0:45–1:10 | Investment Hub
*Action:* Click on 'Investments' in the navigation menu (`/app/investments`). Pause and show the hub.
*Narration:* "The Investment Hub consolidates portfolio, market, risk, and investment insights into one investment experience."

### 1:10–1:40 | Portfolio
*Action:* Click on 'Portfolio' in the sub-navigation (`/app/investments/portfolio`). Show portfolio KPIs, holdings, P&L, and allocation.
*Narration:* "The portfolio experience adapts the useful PortfolioLedger concept from TradeFlow while using the Finance Advisor's own architecture and design system."

### 1:40–2:10 | Market Intelligence
*Action:* Click on 'Market' in the sub-navigation (`/app/investments/market`). Show market news, sentiment, and trend visualization.
*Narration:* "Market Intelligence combines news, sentiment, and trend visualization. Random simulation behavior from the original prototype was removed."

### 2:10–2:35 | Risk & Sentiment
*Action:* Click on 'Risk' in the sub-navigation (`/app/investments/risk`). Hover over risk score, volatility, beta, exposure, and sentiment.
*Narration:* "Risk and sentiment are presented together, with a reusable sentiment visualization shared across the investment experience."

### 2:35–3:15 | Investment Insights
*Action:* Click on 'Insights' in the sub-navigation (`/app/investments/insights`). Interact with the asset selector and show the reasoning and key considerations.
*Narration:* "TradeFlow's DecisionMatrix concept was transformed into structured investment analysis, while OracleStream became a deterministic AI reasoning interface. These insights currently use deterministic mock data. They are not live LLM-generated financial recommendations."

### 3:15–3:40 | Return to Dashboard
*Action:* Navigate back to the Dashboard (`/app/dashboard`) and show the Investment Snapshot again.
*Narration:* "The detailed investment experience remains separated from the main dashboard, while the dashboard provides a concise investment overview."

### 3:40–4:10 | Architecture (PPT Slide 4)
